ABOUT

Mirazul Tanam is an architecture student, Designer and Artist based in Bangladesh.

A practice that moves between spaces, images and stories. Driven by curiosity about how we live, what we see, and what we remember.

I also worked as a creative producer on the documentary Pankauri — exploring another way to tell stories.
